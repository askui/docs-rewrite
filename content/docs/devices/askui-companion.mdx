The **AskUI Companion** lets you test a physical iPhone. It receives the
iPhone's screen image and sends taps and keystrokes to the iPhone over USB.
AskUI Desktop connects to the AskUI Companion over your network. Nothing has
to be installed on the iPhone, and no Xcode or developer mode is needed.

The AskUI Companion is delivered pre-configured by AskUI. You connect three
cables and add it as a device.

**What you need:**

- the AskUI Companion, its power supply and Ethernet cable;
- the iPhone you want to test, with **AssistiveTouch** enabled;
- a computer with AskUI Desktop that can reach the AskUI Companion over the
  network;
- the AskUI Companion's IP address, provided by AskUI.

## Quick start

1. **Enable AssistiveTouch on the iPhone.** Settings → Accessibility →
   Touch → AssistiveTouch → on. A round floating button appears on the
   screen. The agent controls the iPhone through this pointer.
2. **Connect the cables.** Connect the Ethernet cable to your network and the
   USB-C cable of the AskUI Companion to the iPhone. If the iPhone asks
   whether to allow the accessory, tap **Allow**. Plug in the power supply
   (**48V AUX IN**) **last**; the AskUI Companion then starts by itself. All
   other cables are pre-wired, leave them as they are.
3. **Add the device.** In AskUI Desktop, open **Devices** → **Add device**,
   expand **Advanced setups** and pick **A device via Remote Companion
   Controller**.
4. **Fill in the form**, see the table below, and click **Add device**.
   Leave **Capture settings** unchanged.
5. **Probe and connect.** On the Devices page, the new card is named
   `<name> (via RCC)`. Click **Probe** to check that AskUI Desktop reaches the
   AskUI Companion, then **Connect**.

The iPhone now appears in every test's **Run on** picker.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | A name you recognise, e.g. `iPhone 16` |
| **Description**, **Prompt** | Optional, as for every [device](/docs/devices#create-a-profile) |
| **Device** | The model of the connected iPhone. **Viewport width** and **Viewport height** fill in by themselves. Pick **Other iPhone** for a model the list lacks and enter its screen size in points |
| **AskUI Companion address** | The IP address provided by AskUI |
| **AskUI Companion port** | `27000`, do not change it |
| **Capture settings** | Pre-configured, do not change them. Changed values make taps land in the wrong place or cut off the screen image |

## Run it

1. **Connect** the device card.
2. Pick it in the test's **Run on** picker and press **Run**.

Keep the iPhone unlocked with its screen on during a run. The agent acts on
the real screen, as a person with a mouse and keyboard would.

Runs on the AskUI Companion start from AskUI Desktop. The
[CLI](/docs/running-tests/cli) does not drive it yet.

## What the agent can do

- **Tap and type.** A tap goes to a point on the screen; text is typed on the
  keyboard.
- **Swipe and scroll, slowly.** A swipe starts about a second after the press
  and moves in steps. It scrolls lists and drags, and closes apps in the App
  Switcher, but it cannot flick. Write steps that need a fast gesture
  differently, or expect them to fail.
- **Home and App Switcher** go through the AssistiveTouch menu, so keep
  AssistiveTouch enabled.
- **No multi-touch:** no pinch-to-zoom, no two-finger gestures, no rotation.
- **No shortcuts into the app:** no launching by bundle ID, no deep links, no
  view hierarchy. The agent opens apps from the Home Screen or the search,
  like a person.
- **No biometrics:** Face ID cannot be passed. Use a passcode or a test
  account without Face ID.
- The screen image lags slightly behind the iPhone. Every tap, swipe and
  keystroke waits a moment for the screen to settle.

## One device at a time on this computer

The AskUI Companion uses the same AgentOS controller on this computer as the
**Local** device. While one of them is connected, connecting the other is
refused with a message; disconnect the first. On Windows with the AskUI
AgentOS service installed, the Local device runs on the service and can stay
connected.

When **Connect** fails, the card offers **Force Restart**, which stops the
controller on this computer and starts a fresh one.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Probe** fails, or **Connect** fails | The AskUI Companion has power (48V AUX IN). The Ethernet cable is plugged in at both ends. Your computer is in the same network. Address and port `27000` are entered correctly |
| No screen image, or a black image | The USB-C cable is plugged into the iPhone. The iPhone's screen is on and unlocked |
| Taps or the pointer have no effect | AssistiveTouch is enabled. The USB-C cable is plugged into the iPhone, and the iPhone allowed the accessory |
| Taps land in the wrong place | The right iPhone model is selected under **Device**. **Capture settings** are unchanged |

If the problem persists, contact [support@askui.com](mailto:support@askui.com)
with the device name and a short description of what you see.
