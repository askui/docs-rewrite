---
title: Sign in & onboarding
description: Create an AskUI account, set up your workspace, and connect AskUI Desktop and the CLI.
---

AskUI Desktop and the CLI sign in with your AskUI account. Your account belongs
to a **workspace** — the unit that owns your access tokens, usage, and billing.
You manage all of this in the **AskUI Hub**.

## Create your account

<Steps>
<Step>
**Sign up** at the AskUI Hub with your email.
</Step>
<Step>
**Accept the terms and privacy policy.** You can't continue without
consenting — this is a one-time step.
</Step>
<Step>
**Verify your email.** We send a verification link; open it to activate your
account. (You can resend it from the prompt if it doesn't arrive.)
</Step>
<Step>
**Land in the Hub.** The Hub is onboarding-first — it guides you through
setup, then becomes your reference for **tokens** and **settings**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Note">
Authentication uses Auth0 (OIDC). The same login works across AskUI Desktop,
the CLI, and the Hub.
</Callout>
