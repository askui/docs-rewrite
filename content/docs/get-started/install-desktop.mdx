---
title: Install AskUI Desktop
description: Download and install the AskUI Desktop app on Windows or macOS.
---

The entry point for authoring and running AI-driven UI tests. Runs on Windows
and macOS.

<Steps>

<Step>
**Download the installer** for your platform:

<Cards>
  <Card
    title="Windows (x64)"
    href="https://files.askui.com/releases/AskUIDesktop/latest/AskUI-Desktop-latest-Installer-Win-AMD64.exe"
    description="Signed, self-contained."
  />
  <Card
    title="macOS (Apple Silicon)"
    href="https://files.askui.com/releases/AskUIDesktop/latest/AskUI-Desktop-latest-Installer-Mac-ARM64.dmg"
    description="Notarized."
  />
</Cards>

Also on the [AskUI download page](https://www.askui.com/start) (always
newest) and your [AskUI Hub](https://hub.askui.com) account.
</Step>

<Step>
**Run the installer.**

<Tabs items={['Windows', 'macOS']}>
  <Tab value="Windows">Run the `.exe` and accept the prompts.</Tab>
  <Tab value="macOS">Open the `.dmg` and drag AskUI Desktop to Applications.</Tab>
</Tabs>
</Step>

<Step>
**Launch and sign in** with your AskUI account. No account yet? Choose **Create
account**: it opens AskUI's sign-up page in your browser. On first launch the
onboarding wizard sets up permissions and your first project.
</Step>

<Step>
**Run [your first test](/docs/get-started/first-run).**
</Step>

</Steps>
