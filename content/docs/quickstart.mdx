Find your first bug in 10 minutes. This guide takes you from a blank machine to
your first test run, no selectors, no code, just plain language. Four steps:
download AskUI Desktop, sign in, walk the onboarding wizard, and run the
template's example test on your own machine.

<Callout title="What you need">
A Windows or macOS machine. No prior automation experience required.
</Callout>

---

## Step 1: Download AskUI Desktop (2 min)

Grab the installer for your platform and run it. It's self-contained, no
extra runtimes needed.

## Step 2: Sign up or log in (2 min)

Launch AskUI Desktop and sign in with your AskUI account.

<Steps>
<Step>
**New to AskUI?** Click **Sign up** to create an account with your work email,
accept the terms, and verify your email from the link we send. Accounts and
billing live in the [AskUI Hub](https://hub.askui.com).
</Step>
<Step>
**Already have an account?** Click **Log in** and authenticate in your browser.
The same login works across Desktop, the CLI, and the Hub.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Note">
Authentication uses Auth0 (OIDC) and opens in your system browser.
</Callout>

---

## Step 3: Walk the onboarding wizard (3 min)

On first launch, AskUI Desktop opens a short setup wizard.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Welcome.** A quick tour of what AskUI does, click **Get started**.
</Step>
<Step>
**Grant system permissions** *(macOS only).* Allow **Screen Recording** and
**Accessibility** so the agent can see the screen and control input. The status
flips green automatically once granted.
</Step>
<Step>
**Create your first project, Start from template.** Choose **Start from
template** (recommended) and pick an empty folder. AskUI scaffolds the default
template: `tests/` (with `ui.md` and `rules.md`), `procedures/`, and `utils/`, pre-filled with an
example test and the context files (`ui.md`, `rules.md`), ready to run.
</Step>
<Step>
**You're set.** The final screen maps out where everything lives. Click
**Start testing** to open your new project.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 4: Run your first test locally (3 min)

The default template ships with a runnable example test, run it against your
own machine to see a full pass end to end.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Open the example test** in `tests/`. It's plain Markdown, readable steps, no
code or selectors. Leave it as-is for this first run.
</Step>
<Step>
**Run it locally.** Start the run against your local machine (this computer).
AskUI Desktop launches the agent, which acts on the screen step by step and
streams progress into **Live Run**.
</Step>
<Step>
**Read the report.** Each step shows its status (`PASSED`, `FAILED`, `WARN`,
`SKIPPED`, or `BROKEN`) and a screenshot. A green run means every step actually
passed, the agent doesn't improvise its way to done.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Make it yours">
Once the example passes, open `tests/ui.md` on the Tests page and describe your own
app, its main screens, how to tell you're signed in, and any quirks. It's the
single most important file for reliable runs. See
[Prompting best practices](/docs/best-practices/prompting-best-practices).
</Callout>
