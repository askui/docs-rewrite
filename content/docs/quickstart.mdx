Find your first bug in 10 minutes. This guide takes you from a blank machine to
your first test run, no selectors, no code, just plain language. Four steps:
download AskUI Desktop, sign in, walk the onboarding wizard, and run the
demo project's test on your own machine.

<Callout title="What you need">
A Windows or macOS machine. No prior automation experience required.
</Callout>

---

Grab the installer for your platform and run it. It's self-contained, no
extra runtimes needed.

## Step 2: Sign up or log in (2 min)

Launch AskUI Desktop and sign in with your AskUI account.

<Steps>
<Step>
**New to AskUI?** Click **Create account** to create an account with your work email,
accept the terms, and verify your email from the link we send. Accounts and
billing live in the [AskUI Hub](https://hub.askui.com).
</Step>
<Step>
**Already have an account?** Click **Sign in** and authenticate in your browser.
The same login works across Desktop and the Hub. The CLI authenticates with
an [access token](/docs/account-billing/workspaces) instead.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Note">
Authentication uses Auth0 (OIDC) and opens in your system browser.
</Callout>

---

## Step 3: Walk the onboarding wizard (3 min)

On first launch, AskUI Desktop opens a short setup wizard.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Welcome.** Click **Get set up**.
</Step>
<Step>
**Grant system permissions** *(macOS only).* Allow **Screen Recording** and
**Accessibility** so the agent can see the screen and control input. The status
flips green automatically once granted.
</Step>
<Step>
**Where do you want to test?** Tick **Web**, **Desktop** and/or **Android**.
Whatever this computer already has is ticked for you. Ticking a kind starts
setting up what it needs right away, in the background, with its progress on the
card: the Chromium browser for Web (about 170 MB), AgentOS for Desktop, and, once
you accept Google's licenses, the Android SDK and an emulator (about 1–1.5 GB). You
can continue while it downloads.
</Step>
<Step>
**Which model runs your tests?** Signed in with an AskUI account, pick one of the
AskUI models. We recommend starting with **Claude Haiku**: it is fast, costs the
least and runs the demo test well. With a license, check the model you connected
during license setup, or connect another one. The
[AskUI model guide](https://www.askui.com/downloads/askui-ai-models-inference-guide.pdf)
helps you choose. Change it any time under **Settings → AI Settings → Testing Model**.
</Step>
<Step>
**Your workspace** *(AskUI account only).* The wizard names the workspace you work
in. Your plan, credits and billing live in it, you can invite teammates to it, and
your access tokens belong to it. Every new account starts on a free trial with full
access to every feature; the step shows its credits and when it ends. When the
credits run out, runs stop with a billing error until you upgrade on the AskUI Hub.
There is nothing to activate: click **Continue**. **See plans** (or **Manage
subscription** on a paid plan) opens the workspace's plan page on the Hub.
</Step>
<Step>
**You're ready to test.** Watch the short quick start video, or open the tutorial
series. Then click **Start with the demo project**: AskUI creates it in
`Documents/AskUI demo project` (or **Choose another folder**) and opens it.
**Skip the demo** opens the app without a project.
</Step>
</Steps>

The demo project tests the AskUI website. Its first device is the web browser, or
this computer when you ticked Desktop, so its test runs without any setup.

## Step 4: Follow Getting started (5 min)

The dashboard walks you to your first passing test:

- A short **tour** shows where things are: Getting started, Tests, Devices and
  Runs (**Next**, or **Skip tour**).
- **Getting started** shows four steps and the next one to do: **Open the demo
  test**, **Run the test**, **Get it to pass**, **Look at the results**. Its
  button takes you there. On every other page the same steps sit in a small card
  at the bottom right, which you can fold away.
- On the test, **Run** is highlighted. While the test runs, Getting started says
  so; when it ends, **View results** opens its report.
- On the Runs page a short tour explains the run's reports, from the top of the
  list: the conversation log, the summary report, the Developer Report, the
  execution report and Export (**Next**, or **Skip tour**).
- **Setting up your devices** on the dashboard shows the downloads still running,
  with the time left, and **Retry** or **Resume** when one stopped. The same
  downloads show under **Settings → This machine**.

To walk the wizard and Getting started again, open **Settings → This machine →
Setup assistant**.

<Callout title="Make it yours">
Once the demo test passes, open `tests/ui.md` on the Tests page and describe your own
app, its main screens, how to tell you're signed in, and any quirks. It's the
single most important file for reliable runs. See
[Prompting best practices](/docs/best-practices/prompting-best-practices).
</Callout>

<Callout title="Automating another computer?">
For the computer AskUI Desktop runs on, the onboarding sets up **AgentOS**, the
runtime that controls the screen, keyboard and mouse, when you tick **Desktop**
(later: **Settings → This machine → Computer**). Install AgentOS yourself only to
automate a remote computer, a VM or a CI runner, to run with elevated rights, or to
keep the session running on a machine that boots to the login screen. The
[OS Service installer](/docs/agentos/installation/service) covers these on Windows.
</Callout>
