An **integration** is an external account your project uses. Today that is
an e-mail account. You connect it once, and the project can then use it in
two places:

- **Tools**: the **Read Email** and **Send Email** [tools](/docs/extending/tools)
  let a test read recent messages and send messages from the account.
- **Triggers**: a test plan starts when a matching email arrives.

## Connect an e-mail account

1. Open **Extending → Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect Account**.
3. Pick your **Mail provider**: Gmail, Outlook.com / Office 365, Yahoo Mail,
   iCloud Mail or Fastmail. The IMAP and SMTP servers are filled in for you.
   For any other provider, pick **Custom** and enter the servers, ports and
   username yourself.
4. Enter the **Email address** and, optionally, a **Display name**.
5. Enter the **Password / app-password**. Most providers require an
   app-password here, not the password you sign in with. For Gmail, see
   [below](#gmail-app-password).
6. Click **Connect**.

AskUI tests the connection before it saves the account. The test can take
up to two minutes. The password is stored encrypted on this machine, never
in the project folder, so a colleague who opens the project connects with
their own credentials.

## Gmail: use an app-password [#gmail-app-password]

Gmail does not accept your normal Google password from a mail client. If you
enter it, the connection test fails with a message from Google like:

```
Application-specific password required: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833
```

Create an app-password and use it instead:

1. Turn on **2-Step Verification** for the Google account, if it is not on
   already. Google offers app-passwords only with 2-Step Verification.
2. Open [myaccount.google.com/apppasswords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords)
   while signed in with that account.
3. Enter a name (e.g. `AskUI`) and click **Create**.
4. Copy the 16-character password Google shows and paste it into
   **Password / app-password** in AskUI. Spaces don't matter.

Google shows the app-password only once. If you lose it, delete it on the
same page and create a new one.

If the page says app-passwords are not available, the account is probably
a Google Workspace account whose administrator has turned them off, or one
enrolled in Advanced Protection. Ask your administrator, or use a different
account for testing.
