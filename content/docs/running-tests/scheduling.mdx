---
title: Scheduling recurring runs
description: Run a test or plan automatically — daily at a time or every N minutes — while AskUI Desktop is open.
---

A schedule runs a test or [plan](/docs/writing-tests/plans) on
a device profile without you pressing Run.

## Create a schedule

1. Open the test on the **Tests** page (or the plan on **Plans**).
2. Click the alarm-clock button — the **Scheduled runs** dialog opens,
   prefilled with this file.
3. Pick the **Device profile**.
4. Pick the **Cadence**: **Daily at a time** (with weekdays) or
   **Every N minutes**.
5. Click **Add schedule**.

<img
  src="/docs-rewrite/screenshots/desktop-schedule-dialog.png"

  className="mt-4 max-w-full rounded-md border border-fd-border shadow-sm"
/>

The dialog lists every schedule in the project with its last outcome — the
run's results, or why it was skipped (e.g. the device could not connect).
Schedules live in the project's `schedules.json`.

## Only while the app is open

AskUI Desktop is not a background service. Schedules fire only while the app
runs; a missed firing is not made up. A due schedule never double-fires, even
when a run outlasts its interval.

For unattended runs on a build server, use the
[CLI](/docs/running-tests/cli) with your CI scheduler.
